About Us

Welcome to NegativeMark Calculator.

We built this tool because we understand the immense pressure and anxiety that comes with preparing for high-stakes competitive exams like NEET, JEE Main, UPSC, and SSC. In these exams, knowing the right answer is only half the battle; the other half is mastering the strategy of negative marking.

Too often, students leave the exam hall unsure of their actual score, relying on rough mental math that leads to unnecessary stress. We wanted to change that. Our mission was to create a lightning-fast, 100% accurate, and completely free calculator that requires no sign-ups, no logins, and is completely clutter-free.

But we didn't stop at basic math. We integrated unique features like the Guess Probability Engine and Percentile Estimation to help you simulate exam-day strategies *before* you enter the hall.

Whether you are analyzing your mock test results or predicting your rank after the final exam, we are here to give you the exact numbers you need so you can focus on what truly matters: your preparation.
