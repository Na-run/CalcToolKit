How Does Negative Marking Work?

Negative marking is a system used by testing authorities to discourage blind guessing. For every incorrect answer, a fraction of the total marks is deducted. For example, in the NEET UG exam, a correct answer awards +4 marks, while an incorrect answer deducts -1 mark. In the JEE Main, the scheme is identical (+4 for correct, -1 for incorrect). Our calculator allows you to use these presets or fully customize the rules to match any exam format globally.

How do you manually calculate marks with negative marking?

The formula is: Final Score = (Correct Answers × Marks per Correct) - (Incorrect Answers × Negative Marks per Incorrect). For example, in NEET (+4/-1), if you get 80 correct and 20 wrong: (80 × 4) - (20 × 1) = 320 - 20 = 300 marks.

What is a good accuracy rate in competitive exams?

In highly competitive exams like NEET or JEE, an accuracy rate above 85% to 90% is generally considered excellent and safe for top ranks. If your accuracy is below 70%, attempting too many questions might pull your score down due to negative marking.

How does the Guess Probability Engine work?

Our tool uses Expected Value (EV) math. Assuming 4 options per question, blind guessing gives a 25% chance of a correct answer and a 75% chance of a wrong answer. If the penalty for being wrong outweighs the probability of being right, the engine mathematically advises you to leave the question blank.

How is the 1/4th negative marking calculated?

1/4th negative marking means that 25% of the question's total marks will be deducted for a wrong answer. If a correct answer gives you +4 marks, a wrong answer will deduct -1 mark (which is 1/4th of 4). You can select "1/4" from the fraction dropdown in the calculator.

Does an unattempted question get negative marks?

In 99% of competitive exams (including NEET, JEE, and UPSC), unattempted questions are not penalized. They simply earn 0 marks. However, if your specific exam penalizes unattempted questions, you can set the "Deduction per Unanswered" field to a negative value.
