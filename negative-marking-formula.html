Negative Marking Formula

Understand exactly how negative marking works, with formulas and worked examples.

The Universal Formula

Final Score = (C × P) − (I × N) − (U × D)

Where:

  • C = number of correct answers
  • P = marks awarded per correct answer
  • I = number of incorrect answers
  • N = negative marks deducted per incorrect answer
  • U = number of unattempted questions
  • D = deduction per unattempted question (usually 0)

How to Calculate 1/4 Negative Marking

If an exam uses 1/4 negative marking and a correct answer is worth 4 marks:

Deduction per wrong answer = 4 × (1/4) = 1 mark.

Example: 80 correct, 20 incorrect, 0 unattempted → Score = (80 × 4) − (20 × 1) = 320 − 20 = 300.

How to Calculate 1/3 Negative Marking (UPSC)

UPSC Prelims GS Paper I: +2 for correct, −2/3 (≈0.666) for incorrect.

Example: 60 correct, 15 incorrect, 25 unattempted → Score = (60 × 2) − (15 × 0.666) = 120 − 10 = 110.
